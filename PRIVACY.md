# Pave Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Pave is a Chrome extension that helps you save jobs, autofill applications, tailor resumes, and track your job search. This policy explains what data Pave handles and where it goes.

## Summary

Pave has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Everything you give Pave stays on your computer.

## Data Pave stores on your device

- **Your profile and answers:** name, contact details, work history, skills and answers to application questions that you enter or import.
- **Your resume files:** the resume you upload and tailored resumes Pave generates.
- **Saved jobs:** the title, company, URL and text of job postings you choose to save, plus the application status and history you record.
- **Settings:** your preferences, such as the selected AI model and the folder you picked for saved files.

This data is kept in Chrome's local extension storage and IndexedDB on your device, and in files you save to a folder you choose. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

## How the data is used

Only to provide Pave's features: filling application forms you open, tailoring your resume, showing job details, and reminding you to follow up. Pave reads job pages only on the supported job sites listed in the extension, on sites you enable from the toolbar button, or on a page where you press the Autofill shortcut.

## AI features

AI is optional and runs entirely on your device:
- **Gemini Nano** is built into Chrome and runs locally.
- **Downloadable models** (Qwen, Llama) run on your GPU. When you choose to download one, its model files are fetched from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This request contains no personal data. Hugging Face's own privacy policy applies to that download.

Your profile, resume and job data are never sent to an AI service.

## Sharing

Pave does not sell, share or transfer your data. It does not use your data for advertising, credit or lending decisions, or anything unrelated to helping you apply for jobs.

## Your control

In Settings → **Data & Privacy** you can see what is stored, export it all to a file (optionally encrypted with a password), import it back, or erase everything, including downloaded models. Uninstalling Pave also removes its stored data.
